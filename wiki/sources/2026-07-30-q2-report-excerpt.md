# Quarterly report Q2 2026 (excerpt), 30 July 2026

**Original:** `raw/alpstein/2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt.md` · **Type:** quarterly report excerpt for the [[executive-board]] · **Date:** 30 July 2026 · **Prepared by:** Finance ([[marco-steiner]]) · **Period:** 1 April – 30 June 2026

## Summary

In Q2 2026, revenue of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] was slightly above plan and earnings slightly below, because of higher service costs from three downtime incidents at [[bergland-logistik-ag]] and early recruitment in software development. The service share of revenue rose to 31%. The pricing model for [[alpcare-plus]] (CHF 1,900) is approved, the [[alpcare]] price list (CHF 1,200) is unchanged, and the budget for the [[agentic-ai-service-pilot]] is approved. The launch date of [[alppick-2-0]] will be set at the EB meeting in August; a postponement to Q4 would endanger 41 pre-orders. A credit note to Bergland has not been booked; the decision lies with the EB.

## Key points

- **Hardware:** 34 new AlpPick delivered in Q2, 18 of them to [[bergland-logistik-ag]] (expansion of Halls 3 and 4) and 9 to [[toggenburg-moebel-ag]] (new customer). 41 pre-orders for [[alppick-2-0]]; launch date to be set at the EB meeting in August.
- **Software:** [[alpmind]] licenses grow with the fleet; the interface to third-party robots ([[alpmind-platform]]) is at prototype stage; first test at [[rheintal-pharma-ag]] planned for Q3.
- **Service:** AlpCare contracts grow faster than the fleet. Three downtime incidents at Bergland (May/June) caused special costs of **CHF 62,000**. A credit note to Bergland has **not** been booked; the decision lies with the EB. Pricing model AlpCare Plus (CHF 1,900) approved; AlpCare price list (CHF 1,200) unchanged.
- **Personnel:** 12 new hires, 8 of them in software development; 4 open positions in service field staff still unfilled.
- **Risks:** postponement of AlpPick 2.0 to Q4 would endanger the pre-orders; further downtime would weigh on the margin (navigation error fixed since the patch of 24 June 2026); for the Agentic AI pilot the risk lies in the rules – what may the agent commit to toward customers?
- **Outlook:** full year 2026 unchanged at **CHF 38 million revenue** and **10% EBIT margin**.
