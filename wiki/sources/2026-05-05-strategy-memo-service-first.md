# Strategy memo "Service first", 5 May 2026

**Original:** `raw/alpstein/2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first.md` · **Type:** strategy memo · **Date:** 5 May 2026 · **From:** [[lea-brunner]] · **To:** [[executive-board]], [[board-of-directors]] · **Status:** draft for discussion at the EB meeting of 7 May 2026

## Summary

The memo proposes the strategy [[service-first-strategy]] for 2026–2028: [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] should move from robot manufacturer to operator partner, because the hardware market is getting tighter and the edge lies in operation ([[alpmind]] and [[alpcare]]). By 2028, service should make up 50% of total revenue (today about one third). Three initiatives support this: [[alpcare-plus]], [[alpmind-platform]] and the [[agentic-ai-service-pilot]]. Risks named are loss of focus through [[alppick-2-0]], price pressure, liability for AI and a shortage of service technicians. The memo asks the EB for three decisions on 7 May 2026.

> [!note] Uncertain
> The memo is a **draft for discussion** at the EB meeting of 7 May 2026. The wiki does not yet contain the outcome of that meeting; nothing in it counts as decided.

## Key points

- Market: three new providers from Germany and Poland entered Switzerland in 2025; the hardware market for picking robots is getting tighter.
- Headcount: **140 employees** (as of 5 May 2026).
- Goal: service share of revenue **50% by 2028** (today: about one third).
- [[alpcare-plus]]: response time under 4 h, remote monitoring around the clock, replacement robot within 24 h; target customers pharma and hospitals; proposed price **CHF 1,900** per robot and month; responsible [[jonas-weber]].
- [[alpcare]] price stays at **CHF 1,200** per robot and month.
- [[alpmind-platform]]: AlpMind should also control robots of other manufacturers; first interface for the vehicles of a competitor from Bavaria in use at [[rheintal-pharma-ag]]; responsible [[priya-raman]].
- [[agentic-ai-service-pilot]]: AI agent classifies service tickets, resolves standard cases, hands complex ones to humans; goal response time under 10 minutes; pilot from August 2026; budget CHF 180,000; responsible Jonas Weber with Priya Raman.

## Risks

- Loss of focus: AlpPick 2.0 in the fall pulls attention away from service.
- Price pressure: customers compare AlpCare with pure maintenance contracts, which are significantly cheaper.
- Liability for AI: a wrong commitment by the agent makes Alpstein liable; clear rules needed.
- Personnel: service technicians are hard to find; [[remote-monitoring]] must make up for the shortage.

## Decisions requested (EB, 7 May 2026)

1. Approval of the target of 50% service revenue by 2028.
2. Approval of the pricing model AlpCare Plus.
3. Approval of the pilot budget for Agentic AI (CHF 180,000).
