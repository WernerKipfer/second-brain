# Executive Board minutes, 12 March 2026

**Original:** `raw/alpstein/2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes.md` · **Type:** meeting minutes · **Date:** 12 March 2026, 08:30–10:15, Appenzell (Säntis meeting room)

## Summary

The [[executive-board]] of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] reviewed business performance for January–February 2026: order intake is 12% above the previous year, driven by [[alppick]] among logistics customers, while [[alpcare]] service revenue grows more slowly than planned. The board decided not to adjust the annual budget. It decided to launch [[alppick-2-0]] in September 2026; two pilot robots have been running at [[bergland-logistik-ag]] since February. Customers ask for a service variant with a guaranteed response time under 4 hours, which leads to the [[alpcare-plus]] proposal for May. Recruitment in software development and a [[digital-onboarding]] concept are under way.

## Key points

- Order intake Jan–Feb 2026: +12% vs. previous year; driver AlpPick at logistics customers. AlpCare revenue below plan.
- Headcount: **120 employees** at Appenzell and Buchs SG (as of 12 March 2026).
- **Decision:** no adjustment to the annual budget; next review in June 2026.
- [[alppick-2-0]]: payload 25 kg (instead of 15 kg), navigation without floor markings; two pilot robots at Bergland Logistik AG in Buchs since February 2026.
- Open points AlpPick 2.0: battery life in the cold store 6.5 h (target 8 h); CE certification in progress.
- **Decision:** market launch of AlpPick 2.0 in **September 2026**.
- [[remote-monitoring]] via [[alpmind]] reduces on-site visits by about 30% (statement by Jonas Weber).
- AlpCare price: **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 12 March 2026).
- **Decision:** Jonas Weber and Marco Steiner develop a pricing model for [[alpcare-plus]] by the May meeting.
- Personnel: six open positions in software development, four of them in Buchs; onboarding to be digitized by summer ([[digital-onboarding]]).
- Other: team event in September (organized by the EB assistant); next EB meeting on 7 May 2026.
