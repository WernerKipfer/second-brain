# Email thread Bergland Logistik AG, 16–18 June 2026

**Original:** `raw/alpstein/2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland.md` · **Type:** email thread (export from the service mailbox, 18 June 2026) · **Period:** 16–18 June 2026

## Summary

[[thomas-rueegg]] of [[bergland-logistik-ag]] complains about the third downtime incident in four weeks: four [[alppick]] robots in Hall 3 stood still for almost two hours. [[jonas-weber]] names a navigation software error after the update of 20 May as the cause and announces a patch. He tells the customer that AlpCare now costs CHF 1,450 with a guaranteed response time and that the [[alppick-2-0]] launch is assumed for Q4, not confirmed. [[sandra-koller]] warns internally not to commit to anything before [[marco-steiner]] has seen it, as the pricing is not decided. The customer understands an unclear remark as a promise of compensation and asks for a credit note; Jonas Weber says he did not commit to anything concrete.

## Key points

- 15 June 2026: four AlpPick robots in Hall 3 stood still for almost two hours; third incident in four weeks (according to Thomas Rüegg).
- [[remote-monitoring]] reported the error, but nobody was reachable before 10 a.m.
- Cost of downtime for Bergland: about **CHF 4,000 per hour** in extra costs (Thomas Rüegg's figure, 16 June 2026).
- Cause: error in the navigation software after the update of 20 May 2026; patch in the test phase, rollout announced for the following week (Jonas Weber, 16 June 2026).
- Jonas Weber to the customer: AlpCare "now" costs **CHF 1,450** per robot and month with a guaranteed response time – see contradiction below.
- Jonas Weber to the customer: AlpPick 2.0 launch "currently" assumed for **Q4**, "not yet confirmed" – see contradiction below.
- Sandra Koller: Bergland is the largest customer; a credit note would set a precedent; the AlpCare/AlpCare Plus pricing has not been decided.
- Bergland has planned its expansion for September 2026; Q4 "is a problem" (Thomas Rüegg, 17 June 2026).

> [!warning] Contradiction
> Price of AlpCare: [[jonas-weber]] writes to the customer on 16 June 2026 that AlpCare "now costs CHF 1,450 per robot and month" and includes a guaranteed response time (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). The EB minutes of 12 March 2026 ([[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]) and the strategy memo of 5 May 2026 ([[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]]) give **CHF 1,200**, and the memo proposes the guaranteed response time as a separate product, [[alpcare-plus]], at CHF 1,900. On the same day [[sandra-koller]] writes that the pricing question for AlpCare/AlpCare Plus "has not been decided yet" (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). Not resolved – to be clarified.

> [!warning] Contradiction
> Launch date of AlpPick 2.0: the EB decided on a market launch in **September 2026** (12 March 2026, [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]). On 16 June 2026 [[jonas-weber]] writes to the customer that "we currently assume Q4", which is "not yet confirmed" (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). No source documents a decision to postpone. Not resolved – to be clarified.

> [!note] Uncertain
> Credit note / compensation for Bergland: [[jonas-weber]] wrote "I think we can do something about that. I will see what is possible" (16 June 2026). [[thomas-rueegg]] understood this as a commitment and asked for a credit note by the end of June (17 June 2026). Jonas Weber writes internally that he "did not commit to anything concrete" (18 June 2026). [[sandra-koller]] asked him not to commit to anything before [[marco-steiner]] has seen it. **No decision is documented.** (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
