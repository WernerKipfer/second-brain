# AlpPick 2.0

New generation of the [[alppick]] picking robot. Presented by [[priya-raman]].

## Status (as of 12 March 2026)

- Payload 25 kg (instead of 15 kg); new navigation without floor markings. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Two pilot robots at [[bergland-logistik-ag]] in Buchs since February 2026. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## Decisions

- **Market launch in September 2026** (EB, 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## Open points

- Battery life in the cold store: 6.5 h, target 8 h. Proposed solution by [[priya-raman]] by 30 April 2026 (P-03). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- CE certification in progress. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Customer communication by [[sandra-koller]] by 31 May 2026 (P-01). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

> [!note] Uncertain
> The launch date is the status of 12 March 2026. Check newer sources before quoting it to customers.

## From the strategy memo of 5 May 2026

- Named as a risk for the [[service-first-strategy]]: if AlpPick 2.0 arrives in the fall, it pulls attention away from service. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## From the email thread of 16–18 June 2026

- [[bergland-logistik-ag]] has planned its expansion for September 2026 based on the launch; Q4 "is a problem" for them. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])

> [!warning] Contradiction
> Launch date of AlpPick 2.0: the EB decided on a market launch in **September 2026** (12 March 2026, [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]). On 16 June 2026 [[jonas-weber]] writes to the customer that "we currently assume Q4", which is "not yet confirmed" (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). No source documents a decision to postpone. Not resolved – to be clarified.
