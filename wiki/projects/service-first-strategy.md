# Service first strategy 2026–2028

Proposed strategy of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] by [[lea-brunner]]: from robot manufacturer to operator partner. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

> [!note] Uncertain
> The memo is a **draft for discussion** at the EB meeting of 7 May 2026. The wiki does not yet contain the outcome of that meeting; nothing in it counts as decided.

## Goal

- Service share of total revenue **50% by 2028** (today: about one third, as of 5 May 2026). (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Initiatives

1. [[alpcare-plus]] – responsible [[jonas-weber]]
2. [[alpmind-platform]] – responsible [[priya-raman]]
3. [[agentic-ai-service-pilot]] – responsible [[jonas-weber]] with [[priya-raman]]

(Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Risks

- Loss of focus through [[alppick-2-0]] in the fall; price pressure from cheaper maintenance contracts; liability for AI; shortage of service technicians. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Open points

- Approval of the 50% target by the [[executive-board]] on 7 May 2026: outcome not in the wiki.
