# Remote monitoring

Remote monitoring of robots via [[alpmind]], part of [[alpcare]].

- Reduces on-site visits by about 30% (statement by [[jonas-weber]], 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
