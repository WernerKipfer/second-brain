# AlpPick

Picking robot of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]]. The next generation is [[alppick-2-0]].

## Facts

- Current generation: payload 15 kg. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Main driver of the +12% order intake in Jan–Feb 2026, among logistics customers. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Fleet software: [[alpmind]]; service: [[alpcare]].
