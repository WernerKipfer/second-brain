# Bergland Logistik AG

Customer of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] in Buchs.

## Facts

- Two pilot robots of [[alppick-2-0]] have been running at Bergland Logistik AG in Buchs since February 2026. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## For customer meetings (as of 12 March 2026)

- Pilot customer for AlpPick 2.0; the launch was decided for September 2026 (as of 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## From the email thread of 16–18 June 2026

- Our **largest customer** (according to [[sandra-koller]]). (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Contact: [[thomas-rueegg]], Head of Logistics. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Fleet: at least four [[alppick]] robots in Hall 3 (plus the two AlpPick 2.0 pilot robots above); the total fleet size is not in the wiki. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Third downtime incident in four weeks: on 15 June 2026 four robots in Hall 3 stood still for almost two hours; cause was a navigation software error after the update of 20 May 2026. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Cost of downtime according to the customer: about CHF 4,000 per hour (16 June 2026). (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- [[remote-monitoring]] reported the error, but nobody was reachable before 10 a.m. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Expansion planned for September 2026, based on the AlpPick 2.0 launch. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])

> [!note] Uncertain
> Credit note / compensation for Bergland: [[jonas-weber]] wrote "I think we can do something about that. I will see what is possible" (16 June 2026). [[thomas-rueegg]] understood this as a commitment and asked for a credit note by the end of June (17 June 2026). Jonas Weber writes internally that he "did not commit to anything concrete" (18 June 2026). [[sandra-koller]] asked him not to commit to anything before [[marco-steiner]] has seen it. **No decision is documented.** (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])

> [!warning] Contradiction
> Launch date of AlpPick 2.0: the EB decided on a market launch in **September 2026** (12 March 2026, [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]). On 16 June 2026 [[jonas-weber]] writes to the customer that "we currently assume Q4", which is "not yet confirmed" (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). No source documents a decision to postpone. Not resolved – to be clarified.
