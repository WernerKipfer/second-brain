# AlpCare

Service subscription of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] for [[alppick]] robots. Premium variant in preparation: [[alpcare-plus]].

## Facts

- Price: **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Service revenue grows more slowly than planned (as of 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Uses [[remote-monitoring]] via [[alpmind]]. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## From the strategy memo of 5 May 2026

- Price stays at **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 5 May 2026). (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Risk: customers compare AlpCare with pure maintenance contracts, which are significantly cheaper. (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- AlpCare and [[alpmind]] are named as the company's edge ("not the robot but the operation"). (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## From the email thread of 16–18 June 2026

> [!warning] Contradiction
> Price of AlpCare: [[jonas-weber]] writes to the customer on 16 June 2026 that AlpCare "now costs CHF 1,450 per robot and month" and includes a guaranteed response time (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). The EB minutes of 12 March 2026 ([[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]) and the strategy memo of 5 May 2026 ([[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]]) give **CHF 1,200**, and the memo proposes the guaranteed response time as a separate product, [[alpcare-plus]], at CHF 1,900. On the same day [[sandra-koller]] writes that the pricing question for AlpCare/AlpCare Plus "has not been decided yet" (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). Not resolved – to be clarified.

## From the Q2 report of 30 July 2026

- AlpCare price list **CHF 1,200** per robot and month remains unchanged (as of 30 July 2026). (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])
- Service revenue Q2 2026: CHF 3.0 million (plan 2.8); AlpCare contracts grow faster than the fleet: 297 of 412 robots (72%). (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]])

> [!note] Newer source on the AlpCare price
> The Q2 report of 30 July 2026 states: the pricing model for AlpCare Plus (CHF 1,900) **is approved**; the AlpCare price list (CHF 1,200) **remains unchanged** (Source: [[2026-07-30-q2-report-excerpt]]). The CHF 1,450 that [[jonas-weber]] named to [[bergland-logistik-ag]] on 16 June 2026 does not match this either. The report does not say who approved the price or when. The contradiction stays flagged until it is clarified.
