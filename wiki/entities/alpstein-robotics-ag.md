# Alpstein Robotics AG

Manufacturer of picking robots and our own company. Products: [[alppick]] (robot), [[alpmind]] (fleet software), [[alpcare]] (service subscription). Managed by the [[executive-board]].

## Facts

- Sites: Appenzell and Buchs SG. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Headcount: ~~120 employees~~ (as of 12 March 2026, outdated – see below). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Order intake Jan–Feb 2026: +12% vs. previous year, driven by AlpPick among logistics customers. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Service revenue (AlpCare) grows more slowly than planned (as of 12 March 2026). (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Annual budget 2026: not adjusted; next review in June 2026. (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
